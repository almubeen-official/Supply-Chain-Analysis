**Project Code:** CDACL-003
**Role:** Data Analyst Intern
**Tools:** MySQL | Power BI
**Dataset:** 100 Records | 24 Columns

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations to understand product performance, supplier efficiency, inventory levels, manufacturing operations, shipping performance, and transportation costs.

The objective is to identify operational challenges, discover meaningful insights, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve overall supply chain performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze revenue performance across product categories.
* Evaluate supplier cost, lead time, and defect rates.
* Identify products with low inventory levels.
* Compare shipping carrier performance.
* Analyze transportation modes and shipping costs.
* Evaluate manufacturing costs and lead times.
* Understand quality inspection results.
* Identify high-cost transportation routes.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

* **Skincare** generated the highest revenue of 241,628.16.
* **Supplier B** had the lowest cost per unit at INR 45.
* **Carrier B** recorded the lowest average shipping time of 5.30.
* **Haircare** had the highest average defect-rate value of 2.48.
* **Road transportation** recorded the shortest average shipping time of 4.72.
* **Route B** had the highest average route cost of 595.66.
* The low-stock analysis identified 17 records with stock levels of 10 or fewer.
* Inspection results showed 41% Pending, 36% Fail, and 23% Pass.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Investigate higher defect rates in haircare products.
* Review supplier performance using cost, quality, and delivery time together.
* Monitor low-stock products and prioritize inventory checks.
* Improve inspection follow-up for pending and failed records.
* Evaluate transportation costs, especially Route B.
* Review manufacturing delays in the haircare category.

## ⚠️ Project Limitations

* The main dataset contains 100 records.
* The separate Supplier Performance table contains three records.
* Supplier names across the two tables were not joined because a verified mapping was unavailable.
* Findings are based on the supplied dataset and are descriptive, not forecasts.
